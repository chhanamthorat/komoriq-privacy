Komoriq · Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026 · Applies to Komoriq version 0.8.1 and later (package com.still.bonsai)

This policy explains how the Komoriq Android app ("Komoriq", "the app") handles information. Komoriq is published by chhanamthorat ("the publisher", "we").

Summary

Komoriq does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Your focus data stays on your device

Komoriq stores completed focus sessions, daily counts, streaks, personal bests, achievements and preferences locally on your device. It also temporarily stores your previous Do Not Disturb settings when needed to restore them after a session. No account is required, and there is no sign-in.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, cloud account or developer-operated backend. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send your focus records to us or to any other company. Fonts, artwork and the garden are bundled with the app and work offline.

Permissions and Strict mode

With your permission, Strict mode uses Android's Do Not Disturb controls (the notification policy access permission) to suppress non-call interruptions, and Android screen pinning to discourage leaving a session. Calls are allowed by the app's Do Not Disturb policy; other Android modes, volume and manufacturer settings can still affect them.

The app does not read the contents of your notifications, call logs, contacts, messages or phone numbers, and it does not request camera, microphone or location access. Strict mode is optional and can be turned off before you start a session.

When controls are released

The app releases its strict-session controls when the timer finishes or the bonsai fully wilts. Android's own unpin, emergency and power controls always remain available. If the app is interrupted, it attempts to recover. On older Android versions it restores your previous Do Not Disturb policy; on Android 15 and later it deactivates its own Do Not Disturb rule.

Retention and deletion

Focus records remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Preferences and achievements are removed with that local data. There is no server account or cloud copy held by us, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can also adjust or revoke Do Not Disturb access at any time in Android's system settings.

Children

Komoriq does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not receive any. If you install Komoriq through Google Play, Google may process information about your download and device under Google's own privacy policy; that is outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected before they take effect in a published release.
